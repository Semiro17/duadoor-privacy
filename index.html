DuaDoor — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026

DuaDoor does not collect any data. Not names, not email addresses, not usage, not analytics, not crash reports. There is no account to create and no server to send anything to. Everything the app knows stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • The apps you choose to guard. DuaDoor uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls). When you pick apps, Apple gives DuaDoor opaque tokens — DuaDoor never receives, and cannot read, the names or icons of the apps you select. These tokens are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • Your onboarding answers, streak, unlock window, and Daily Wird counts. Stored locally in the app's own container on your device.
  • Notification preferences. Whether you allowed notifications is decided by iOS and read by the app; nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. DuaDoor checks your entitlement on your device using Apple's StoreKit. DuaDoor does not see your payment details and does not run a server that records your purchase.

No tracking, no third parties

DuaDoor contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is Apple's own App Store and StoreKit traffic.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything DuaDoor stored is removed with it, and iOS removes the Screen Time shields the app set.

Children

DuaDoor uses the individual Screen Time authorization for the person using the device. It is not designed to manage another person's device and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes
